## Site architecture and buyer intent

Home introduces the agency, RUN / GROW / BUILD, the support models, real team work and commercial options. All seven service URLs are permanently linked in the footer and initial non-JavaScript HTML. Group panels remain in the rendered DOM, even when another group is selected.

| Service URL under `/allo-society/services/` | Primary question | Scope boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `administration-operations/` | What recurring tasks can an assistant own? | Define capacity, access and escalation; no direct-hire placements |
| `websites/` | What website work can Allo deliver? | Agree pages, functions and delivery scope; not unlimited engineering |
| `social-media/` | What planning, publishing and community work is included? | Agree channels, content volume and production responsibilities |
| `digital-ads/` | What campaign support does Allo provide? | Media spend and support fees are separate; no guaranteed returns |
| `crm-automation/` | What workflows can be organized or automated? | Define supported tools, access and integration requirements |
| `dashboards/` | What reporting can the business use to decide? | Scope sources, metrics, refresh needs and data quality |
| `internal-tools-erp/` | What internal tools can be configured or connected? | Platform configuration and workflow support, not unrestricted bespoke ERP development |

Allo Assistant, Allo Executive and Allo Specialist describe support responsibility, not extra top-level service categories. Allo Hire is excluded under the current master direction. Monthly Support, Fixed Project and Custom ALLO. describe commercial arrangements. Do not invent price amounts, availability, service guarantees, reviews or certifications.

## Implemented controls

- Unique titles, descriptions and self-referencing canonical URLs for the homepage and seven service documents.
- Static service content, one H1 per page, understandable scope sections and native expandable FAQs.
- FAQ JSON-LD regenerated from the visible questions and answers. Service identity, provider relationship and two-level breadcrumbs included.
- Six related-service links on every service page; genuine team portfolio work remains within Allo, with attribution and recorded status preserved.
- Sitemap lists the eight canonical pages. Last-modified dates represent this substantive revision.
- Public indexing meta directives retained. The project-level `robots.txt` is distributed, but crawler policy is controlled by the **host-root** `/robots.txt`, not `/allo-society/robots.txt`. Verify host-root access when changing hosting; do not treat a project-subfolder file as an effective robots policy.
- Official supplied outlined logo and favicon, without a replacement symbol or reliance on an installed logo font.
- Responsive readable content and keyboard/touch access. Selection controls do not replace crawlable service links.
