ANSWER LIBRARY

Customer support questions

How does the assistant know when to escalate?

Response boundaries, issue categories, and escalation rules are agreed during setup and refined as the assistant learns the business.

Can responses follow our brand voice?

Yes. Existing examples, tone guidance, and approved responses are used as working context. New or sensitive situations can stay draft-first.

Is this a full customer service department?

No. It is dedicated virtual assistance for defined recurring support tasks and clear escalation, shaped around the agreed weekly capacity.
