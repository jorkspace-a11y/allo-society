Inbox support questions

Will my assistant send replies without approval?

The approval and escalation rules are agreed during setup. You can begin with draft-first support, then delegate routine replies only when the boundaries are clear.

Can you work inside my existing email setup?

Yes. The workflow is shaped around the tools, folders, labels, and response rules already used by your business.

Can inbox support be combined with other tasks?

Yes. Inbox management can be combined with calendar, operations, or customer support within the agreed weekly support rhythm.
