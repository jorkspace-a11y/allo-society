ANSWER LIBRARY

Operations support questions

Can an Allo Assistant work inside our existing tools?

Yes. The role is shaped around the tools and working routines already used by your business rather than forcing a new system without context.

Can the assistant improve a process?

Your assistant can document friction and suggest clearer repeatable steps. Material process changes remain your decision.

What should we delegate first?

Start with a repeatable task that has a clear input, expected output, and escalation point. The delegation audit can help identify the strongest first handoff.
