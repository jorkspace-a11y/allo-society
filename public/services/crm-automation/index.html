CRM and automation questions

Can ALLO. work with an existing CRM?

Yes. The scope can begin with the existing platform, pipeline, forms, and workflow before deciding what should change.

Can ongoing support follow the setup?

Yes. A fixed setup project can be followed by optional maintenance or optimisation support.

Does automation replace every manual step?

No. The goal is to automate suitable repeatable work while preserving human decisions and clear exception handling.
