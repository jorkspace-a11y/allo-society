ANSWER LIBRARY

Calendar support questions

How are scheduling conflicts handled?

Your availability rules and priorities are agreed during setup. Conflicts outside those rules are escalated instead of being resolved by assumption.

Can my assistant prepare meeting details too?

Yes. Relevant notes, links, participant details, and agreed preparation can be attached to the calendar event before the meeting.

Can calendar support include travel planning?

Yes. Your assistant can research travel options, coordinate itinerary details, and place confirmed plans into your calendar.
